A. MODEL DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standalone models estimate the total association of each provision bundle with the GPR response.
FTA-conditional models include FTA membership and its GPR interaction, so the bundle coefficient measures incremental agreement composition beyond membership.
All five bundles are NOT entered jointly because the bundle correlations are high.

D. INTERPRETATION RULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Emphasize a mechanism only when its direction is stable across both clustering methods and it survives BH-FDR adjustment in the FTA-conditional specification.
A positive interaction indicates a potential buffering channel; a negative interaction indicates greater sensitivity under higher GPR.
