E. MODEL NOTE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Global GPR varies only by year. Its standalone coefficient is absorbed by year fixed effects.
The main estimand is therefore the interaction between GPR and lagged bilateral DTA depth.
Use continuous gpr_mean_z in the baseline. Annual p90 identifies only very few trade-period years and should not be the primary treatment.
